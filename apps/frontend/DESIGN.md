# Share images

Caelestis beside its name. The subtitle names the site's purpose. Keep the composition legible at
thumbnail size, with a 1200 × 630 canvas for the default image. Template GIFs devote their entire
640 × 360 canvas to the artwork; the embed title and description carry its name and progress.
