Show other online painters over the artwork as see-through shapes in the colour Wplace shows for them: a dashed, dotted viewport while browsing, a solid, striped viewport plus their drafted pixels while painting, and solid, striped region claims; with settings to share your own position and to hide the indicators. A tab opens one presence socket, on the connected server with the fewest painters online, so busy servers carry fewer collaboration sockets and painters who share several servers are not shown twice; claims saved on the other servers still show, fetched over HTTP.
- Claim a region in a new claim mode, an Illustrator-style vector editor over the map: a tool drawer with selection, direct selection, pen, pencil, brush, rectangle, ellipse, polygon, and star, a confirm-or-cancel bar on top, and every shape and stroke staying editable with handles and bezier curves. Shapes add to or subtract from the claim. The map keeps panning and zooming throughout. Claims rasterise to whole pixels with no anti-aliasing and do not need a template underneath. Enter from the shapes button on the rail or M, and edit a saved claim from the Painters drawer. Q hides and shows other painters and every claim.
- The "In progress" drawer is now "Favourites", and a separate "Painters" drawer shows who is online and the region claims.
- The Wplace theme toggle moves from L to N, and works again: it calls Wplace's own theme setter, and if that cannot be reached it drives the settings-dialog control without showing it, or sets the theme and map style directly.
