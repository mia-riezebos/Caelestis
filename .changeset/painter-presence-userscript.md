Show other online painters over the artwork as see-through shapes in the colour Wplace shows for them: a dashed, dotted viewport while browsing, a solid, striped viewport plus their drafted pixels while painting, and solid, striped region claims; with settings to share your own position and to hide the indicators. A tab opens one presence socket, on the connected server with the fewest painters online, so busy servers carry fewer collaboration sockets and painters who share several servers are not shown twice; claims saved on the other servers still show, fetched over HTTP. Viewports are sent only when you pan or zoom, at most every 300 ms while moving, and glide to each new position over the same 300 ms with an ease-out instead of jumping. Name tags appear only while the pointer is over a viewport, a draft, or a claim, centred above it; a claim made of several separate pieces tags each piece, merging neighbours whose tags would land on each other or on another piece until you zoom in, and climbing clear of other painters' claims.
- Claim a region in a new claim mode, an Illustrator-style vector editor over the map: a tool drawer of grouped buttons (hold or right-click one for the tools behind it) with selection, direct selection, lasso, pen, pencil, paintbrush, rectangle, ellipse, polygon, star, and hand, Illustrator's keys (V, A, Q, P, N, B, M, L, H, Space to pan), a confirm-or-cancel bar on top, and every shape and stroke staying editable with handles and bezier curves. Select several shapes with Shift-click, a marquee, or the lasso, and move or delete them together. Direct selection edits the anchors of every shape (a rectangle, ellipse, polygon, or star becomes a path when you drag one) and rotates a shape from a grip above it or from just outside a corner, with Shift snapping to 15°. In claim mode the wheel pans, Shift+wheel pans sideways, and Alt/Option or Ctrl/Cmd+wheel zooms. Shapes add to or subtract from the claim. Claims rasterise to whole pixels with no anti-aliasing and do not need a template underneath. Enter from the shapes button on the rail or M, and edit a saved claim from the Painters drawer. Q hides and shows other painters and every claim.
- The "In progress" drawer is now "Favourites", and a separate "Painters" drawer shows who is online and the region claims.
- The Wplace theme toggle moves from L to N, and works again: it calls Wplace's own theme setter, and if that cannot be reached it drives the settings-dialog control without showing it, or sets the theme and map style directly.
