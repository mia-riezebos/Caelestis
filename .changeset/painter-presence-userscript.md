Show other online painters' drafts and claimed regions under the artwork and their viewports as dashed outlines over it, with settings to share your own position and to hide the indicators.
- Claim a region with a new drawing tool: press M for a rectangle or L for an ellipse, or pick a polygon or star from its toolbar, drag it on the map, click one of your claims to move or reshape it, and Delete removes it. Claims are whole pixels with no anti-aliasing.
- The Wplace theme toggle moves from L to N to make room for the ellipse tool, and works again: it drives Wplace's own theme control through the settings dialog without showing it, and falls back to setting the theme and map style directly if that control changes.
